Church slug login

Tell us your church slug to enter the right tenant

Every church on pi-CMS gets a unique slug, like /grace-community. Your staff and campus leaders will log in using this slug so we can route them to your tenant securely.

Need help?

  • • Ask your superadmin for the slug (shown in Superadmin → Tenant details)
  • • It is included in welcome emails when a church is created
  • • Slug = the URL-safe version of your church name. Example login URL: pisairtel.app/login/grace-community
pisairtel.app/login/

What is a slug?

It is the short, URL-friendly identifier for your church. We create it from your church name during registration so staff can log in securely at pisairtel.app/login/<slug>.
